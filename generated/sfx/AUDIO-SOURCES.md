## Original procedural sound design

The following contain no third-party recordings or samples. They were built
locally from generated oscillators, filtered noise, Karplus-Strong plucks and
deterministic envelopes specifically for this game:

- `tool-hoe.mp3`
- `tool-water.mp3`
- `tool-fertilise.mp3`
- `tool-pesticide.mp3`
- `tool-pick.mp3`
- `harvest.mp3`
- `feed-animals.mp3`
- `animal-duck.mp3`
- `animal-chicken.mp3`
- `animal-cow.mp3`
- `craft-finished.mp3`
- `level-up.mp3`
- `new-morning.mp3`
- `rain-overnight.mp3`
- `pest-appears.mp3`
- `refused.mp3`
- `ambient-farm-day.mp3`
- `ambient-village-shop.mp3`

The two ambient beds are 32-second deterministic loops. Their synthesis uses
integer-cycle periodic layers and an additional circular seam blend; they do
not contain a downloaded music composition or sound recording.
